summary:
- This document sets the standard contract obligations applying to all supplier agreements held by Meridian Procurement Ltd, including delivery obligations, renewal, price adjustment and termination.
- Supplier contracts renew for a further twelve months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the contract end date.
- Meridian may end an agreement immediately where a supplier enters insolvency proceedings, loses a mandatory certification, or commits a material breach that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice.
