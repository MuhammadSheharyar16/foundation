# DOC-001 — Supplier Selection and Sourcing Policy

**Organisation:** Meridian Procurement Ltd (synthetic)
**Document owner:** Category Management
**Version:** 2.0
**Effective date:** 01 November 2025
**Review cycle:** Annual
**Classification:** Internal — synthetic training data

---

## 1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs how Meridian Procurement Ltd identifies, evaluates and
selects vendors for goods and services. It applies to all sourcing events above
the framework threshold, including new sourcing, re-sourcing and single-source
justifications.

Throughout this document, "supplier" and "vendor" are used interchangeably and
carry the same meaning.

## 2. Approved supplier list

Only suppliers on the approved supplier list may receive a purchase order. The
list is maintained by Category Management and reviewed every twelve months.

The approved supplier list currently includes Nordvale Components Ltd,
Brackenfield Logistics Ltd, Halvorsen Industrial Supply and Petronella Services
Ltd. Additions require a completed sourcing evaluation and sign-off by the Head
of Commercial.

A supplier removed from the list for performance reasons may not be reinstated
for twelve months from the date of removal.

## 3. Evaluation criteria and scoring

Every sourcing option is scored against four weighted criteria:

| Criterion            | Weight | Scored by            |
|----------------------|--------|----------------------|
| Total cost           | 40%    | Category manager     |
| Delivery reliability | 25%    | Supply Chain Ops     |
| Technical compliance | 20%    | Requesting function  |
| Financial stability  | 15%    | Commercial Assurance |

Each criterion is scored out of ten. A sourcing option scoring below six on total
cost or below five on any other criterion is not shortlisted, regardless of its
overall weighted total.

Scores are recorded against the sourcing event reference and retained for six
years.

## 4. Single-source justification

Sourcing without competition is permitted only where the goods are proprietary,
where a compatible replacement would require requalification, or where an urgent
operational need is documented and approved in advance.

A single-source justification must be approved by the Head of Commercial before
the purchase order is raised. Retrospective approval is not accepted.

## 5. Cross-reference to contract terms

Once a sourcing option is selected, the resulting agreement is governed by
DOC-002. Note that this version of the sourcing policy states a ninety day
non-renewal notice period; that figure was superseded by DOC-002 version 1.4 and
DOC-002 takes precedence.

## 6. Sourcing event records

Each sourcing event records the requesting function, the shortlisted options, the
scores awarded, the named category manager who owned the event, and the final
selection rationale. Sourcing records are visible to Category Management and
Commercial Assurance only.
