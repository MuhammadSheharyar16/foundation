## 1. Scope

This document sets the standard contract obligations applying to all supplier
agreements held by Meridian Procurement Ltd, including delivery obligations,
renewal, price adjustment and termination. Where this document conflicts with an
earlier policy, this document takes precedence.

## 2. Delivery obligations

Suppliers must deliver goods within five working days of a confirmed purchase
order. Shipments are accepted at the Meridian receiving bay between 08:00 and
16:00, Monday to Friday. Deliveries outside these hours require written approval
from Supply Chain Operations at least 24 hours in advance.

A delivery is complete only when the goods have been signed for by an authorised
receiving officer and the delivery note reference has been recorded against the
purchase order. Shipments arriving without a valid purchase order number may be
refused at the receiving bay.

## 3. Late, partial and rejected deliveries

If a vendor expects to miss the five working day window, it must notify Meridian
in writing at least 48 hours before the agreed delivery date, stating the revised
date and the reason for the delay.

Partial shipments are permitted only where the purchase order explicitly allows
them. Where a partial delivery is accepted, the outstanding balance must be
delivered within a further three working days.

Repeated late delivery — three or more late deliveries in any rolling 90-day
period — triggers a formal performance review with the assigned category manager.
Goods arriving damaged, incomplete or outside specification are recorded as a
rejection; replacement goods must be delivered within seven working days at the
supplier's cost.

## 4. Renewal and notice

Supplier contracts renew for a further twelve months unless either party gives
written notice of non-renewal at least 60 days before the contract end date.
Notice must be sent to the named commercial contact recorded in the contract
schedule.

Contracts with an annual value above GBP 250,000 require a documented renewal
review before the notice deadline. The review is completed by the category
manager and signed off by the Head of Commercial.

## 5. Price adjustment at renewal

A supplier may request a price adjustment at renewal. Requests must be submitted
in writing no later than 90 days before the contract end date and must include a
cost breakdown supporting the proposed change. Requests made after this deadline
are not considered until the following renewal cycle.

Where no adjustment is agreed, existing contract rates carry forward unchanged
for the renewal term.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate or cancel an agreement for convenience with 90 days
written notice. Meridian may end an agreement immediately where a supplier enters
insolvency proceedings, loses a mandatory certification, or commits a material
breach that is not remedied within 30 days of written notice.
