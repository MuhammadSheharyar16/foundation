# DOC-003 — Pricing, Cost Breakdown and Payment Terms

**Organisation:** Meridian Procurement Ltd (synthetic)
**Document owner:** Finance Operations
**Version:** 1.1
**Effective date:** 01 February 2026
**Classification:** Internal — synthetic training data

---

## 1. Scope

This policy covers how supplier pricing is structured, how costs are broken down
for evaluation, and how invoices are submitted, approved and paid.

## 2. Cost breakdown requirements

Every quoted price must be submitted as a breakdown, not a single figure. The
required cost components are:

| Component        | Description                                          |
|------------------|------------------------------------------------------|
| Unit cost        | Base charge per item or per day of service           |
| Freight          | Transport and handling to the Meridian receiving bay |
| Tooling          | One-off setup, tooling or mobilisation charges       |
| Support          | Warranty, maintenance or helpdesk cover              |

A quotation submitted without a component breakdown is returned to the vendor and
is not scored. Where a component does not apply, the supplier states nil rather
than omitting the line.

## 3. Rate cards and validity

Agreed rates are recorded on a rate card attached to the contract schedule. A
rate card is valid for the contract term and is not adjusted mid-term except
where a change-control note is signed by both parties.

Rate cards for CTR-2024-0117 and CTR-2025-0042 are held in the contract
management register. Rate card contents are restricted to Finance Operations and
the assigned category manager.

## 4. Invoicing

Invoices must quote the purchase order number, the contract reference, the
delivery note reference and the cost components as agreed on the rate card. An
invoice that cannot be matched to a receipted purchase order is rejected and
returned within two working days.

Invoices are submitted to the Finance Operations shared mailbox. Invoices sent
directly to individual buyers are not processed.

## 5. Payment terms

Approved invoices are paid 30 days from the date of receipt of a valid invoice.
Payment is made by bank transfer to the account verified during onboarding.
Changes to supplier bank details require verification by two Finance Operations
staff members before any payment is released.

Late payment may attract interest under applicable legislation. Meridian does not
publish a contractual late payment rate in this policy.

## 6. Disputed charges

Where Meridian disputes a charge, the disputed line is placed on hold and the
undisputed remainder of the invoice is paid on the normal terms. The supplier is
notified of the disputed line within five working days. Disputes not resolved
within 30 days are escalated to the Head of Commercial.

## 7. Currency and taxes

All prices are quoted in GBP unless the contract schedule states otherwise.
Prices are quoted exclusive of VAT, which is shown as a separate line on the
invoice.
